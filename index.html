Manufacturing

From design to delivery.

Knitting, cutting, stitching, finishing, inspection and packing run under our own roof in Ludhiana. We are a manufacturer, not a trading house — which is how quality and delivery stay in our hands.

Stage 01

Product development

Artwork, yarn and shade decided for the season's articles.

Stage 02

Knitting

Computerised flat knitting against the booked quantity.

Stage 03

Cutting

Panels cut to size and prepared for the stitching line.

Stage 04

Stitching

Assembly, overlock and finishing operations on the floor.

Stage 05

Finishing

Washing, pressing and trims bring each piece to its final handle.

Stage 06

Quality inspection

Weight, size, shade and finish checked piece by piece.

Stage 07

Packaging

Article-wise packing with labels prepared for your counter.

Stage 08

Dispatch

Booked through your preferred transport with documents shared.
