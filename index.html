About LAKNITE

A shawl house built on repeat orders, not one-time sales.

LAKNITE is a premium shawl manufacturing and wholesale brand based in Ludhiana, Punjab.

For more than two decades we have supplied retailers, wholesalers and distributors with quality winter shawls designed for Indian markets. Our focus is craftsmanship, dependable supply and long-term business relationships.

Production process

How an order moves through the floor.

Stage 1

Design development

Artwork, yarn and shade decided for the season's articles.

Stage 2

Manufacturing

Knitting and embroidery run against the booked quantity.

Stage 3

Finishing

Washing, pressing and border work bring the piece to its final handle.

Stage 4

Quality inspection

Weight, size, shade and finish checked piece by piece.

Stage 5

Packaging

Article-wise packing with labels prepared for the buyer's counter.

Stage 6

Dispatch

Booked through the buyer's preferred transport with documents shared.
