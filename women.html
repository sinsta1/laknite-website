Women · seasonal collections

Built around the selling season.

Winter does not arrive everywhere at once. The women's range is released in three drops so your counter carries the right weight at the right time.

Drop 01 · October – November

Early Winter

Light drapes and fine knits for the first cool weeks.

  • Fine-gauge stoles
  • Open-front cardigans
  • Single-layer thermals
  • Plain ladies caps
Drop 02 · December – January

Peak Winter

Heavy drapes and the festive occasion range together.

  • Fur-trim capes
  • Embroidered occasion shawls
  • Chunky fringed stoles
  • Jacquard beanies
Drop 03 · February – March

Transition

Lighter weights and carry-forward shades for the tail of the season.

  • Mid-weight ponchos
  • Printed stoles
  • Knitted accessories
  • Carry-forward shades
