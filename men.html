Men · seasonal collections

Built around the selling season.

Winter does not arrive everywhere at once. The men's range is released in three drops so your counter carries the right weight at the right time.

Drop 01 · October – November

Early Winter

Light layers that sell before the cold properly arrives.

  • Hooded gilets
  • Light fleece hoodies
  • Single-jersey thermals
  • Fine-gauge knitwear
Drop 02 · December – January

Peak Winter

The heavy weights, where the season's volume sits.

  • Padded windcheaters
  • Jacquard fleece hoodies
  • Brushed-back thermal sets
  • Woven mufflers
Drop 03 · February – March

Transition

Mid-weight pieces that hold the counter until spring.

  • Half-zip knitwear
  • Sweatshirts
  • Ribbed caps
  • Carry-forward shades
